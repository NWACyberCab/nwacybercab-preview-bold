How this number is calculated

Method: we start from Tesla's last stated total (1,000,000 unsupervised miles on September 3, 2026) and assume growth of 17.5% a week continues: miles = base × (1 + growth)weeks since base. That rate is our own calculation, not a figure Tesla stated. It's the average weekly growth between Tesla's two announced totals: 380,000 miles on July 22 and 1,000,000 on Sept 3, 2026. (On its Q2 call Tesla described weekly mileage growth of "more than 10% a week.") Real growth may be faster or slower, so treat this as an estimate. It covers Tesla's own driverless fleet (mostly Model Y) in the cities where Tesla operates. RoboRide is not yet operating, and none of these are RoboRide miles.

Sources: 1M miles announced by Ashok Elluswamy at Tesla's Cybercab event, Austin, Sept 3, 2026 (TeslAnt, event video) · 380,000-mile total and "more than 10% a week" remark, Q2 2026 earnings call, July 22, 2026 (transcript, Business Insider).

Source: Tesla FSD safety data, miles driven per collision, FSD (Supervised) vs. manual driving.

How it works

How it will work.

RoboRide aims to make getting around NWA effortless: request a Cybercab, hop in, and go. No driver means no small talk and no tip. Booking details will be shared before launch.

  1. 01

    Request a ride or a delivery

    Tell us where you're going, or where your package needs to go.

  2. 02

    Your Cybercab arrives

    A Tesla Cybercab pulls up to your pickup spot. Confirm it is yours and step inside, or load your goods.

  3. 03

    Sit back and arrive

    The Cybercab drives itself to the destination while you follow the trip in real time.

Why Northwest Arkansas

A region that's on the move.

NWA is growing fast, flying more, and home to the state's flagship university. That means more trips between home, work, campus, and the airport, and more goods moving across the region.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is RoboRide operating yet?

Not yet. RoboRide LLC is preparing to launch and no cities are live today. Join the waitlist to hear when service starts in your area.

Where will RoboRide operate?

Northwest Arkansas. The planned service area includes Bentonville, Rogers, Springdale, Fayetteville, Farmington, Siloam Springs, and Eureka Springs. We plan to launch city by city.

What services will you offer?

Two things: driverless rides for people, and local delivery of goods for businesses and customers, both using autonomous Tesla Cybercabs.

How much will it cost?

Pricing hasn't been set yet. Rates will be announced before launch, and waitlist members will hear first.

Is RoboRide part of Tesla?

No. RoboRide LLC is an independent Northwest Arkansas company that plans to operate Tesla Cybercab vehicles. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Inc.

I run a business. Can we work together?

We'd like to hear from you. Choose “Business partnership” on the waitlist form and we'll follow up about delivery, employee rides, or venue pickup zones.

What happens to my email if I join the waitlist?

We use it only to contact you about RoboRide, and we will never sell it. See our Privacy Policy for details.
